Aim

To demonstrate the ability of text-to-image generation tools to reproduce an existing image by crafting precise prompts. The goal is to identify key elements within the image and generate a similar output.

AI Tools Required

ChatGPT, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, MidJourney

Explanation

In this experiment, images are analyzed by identifying objects, colors, lighting, background, and style. Based on this analysis, prompts are created and refined step by step. These prompts are then used in AI image generation tools to reproduce images similar to the original ones. The generated images are compared with the original images to evaluate accuracy.

Image 1 – Sunset Landscape
Original Image

Basic Prompt

A landscape with mountains and a river during sunset.

Refined Prompt

A serene sunset landscape with purple mountains, a calm river reflecting the orange and pink sky, and a few trees along the shore.

Final Prompt

A highly detailed digital painting of a sunset landscape with purple mountains, a calm reflective river, golden-orange sky, soft pastel clouds, trees along the riverbank, cinematic lighting, 4K resolution.

Generated Image

Comparison
Similar colors and lighting observed
River reflection and mountains are accurate
Slight variation in clouds and tree placement
